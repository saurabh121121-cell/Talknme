TalkNMe does not ask you to publish a profile just to have a conversation. We are building the service around minimal information and personal control.

🛡️

Safety matters

Conversations are provided through a moderated service with controls intended to reduce spam and help people end uncomfortable interactions.

Questions, answered.

Do I need to give you my email?

No. The current TalkNMe conversation flow does not require an email to start.

Is TalkNMe a subscription?

No. You choose the amount of conversation time you want and pay according to the available option.

Do I have to use video?

No. TalkNMe is designed around camera-free conversation and voice when you are ready.

Can I stop a conversation?

Yes. You remain in control and can end the conversation when you want.

Are the people real?

TalkNMe is designed for conversations with human listeners. We will not present bots as human listeners.

Is this therapy or professional counselling?

No. TalkNMe is a conversation service, not a substitute for qualified medical, mental-health, legal or emergency support.
